Audit dan lengkapi layanan Company Profile pada Customer Portal agar customer menerima deliverable yang jelas dan end-to-end.

STATUS SAAT INI:

- Brief Assistant tersedia
- Brief Intelligence scoring tersedia
- AI menghasilkan visi, misi, value proposition, deskripsi layanan, dan closing
- PDF document engine tersedia
- PPTX presentation engine tersedia
- Brand Kit dapat menyuntikkan logo dan warna
- Review token tersedia
- Download terkunci oleh payment gate
- Live runtime tracking tersedia

PENTING:

Jangan membuat Document Engine, Presentation Engine, Brand Kit, Review System, Payment Gate, Queue, atau Workflow baru.

Reuse sistem yang sudah ada.

Semua perubahan harus:
- additive
- OpenAPI-first
- type-safe
- backward compatible
- anti-fabrication
- pnpm run verify clean

==========================================================

→ Payment or DP Verified
→ Generate
→ Internal QC
→ Client Review
→ Revision

→ Remaining Payment
