TEAM 30 — JEWELRY DESIGN DOMAIN PLUGIN

BRANCH WAJIB:
feature/team-30-jewelry-plugin

==================================================
TUJUAN
==================================================

Bangun Jewelry Design Plugin.

Plugin mendukung concept sampai production specification metadata tanpa membuat
gemological certification, pricing engine, atau CAD manufacturing engine penuh.

==================================================
WORKFLOW
==================================================

Minimal:

1. Brief
2. Reference Research
3. Style Direction
4. Concept Sketch
5. Form Development
6. Material/Gem Direction
7. Technical View
8. Visualization
9. Production Specification
10. Review
11. Export

==================================================
BRIEF
==================================================

Contoh:

- jewelry type;
- target wearer;
- occasion;
- style;
- dimensions;
- metal preference;
- stone/gem preference;
- finish;
- budget range;
- production method;
- personalization;
- sustainability;
- safety constraints.

==================================================
MATERIAL CONTRIBUTION
==================================================

Contoh metadata:

- metal type;
- purity label;
- finish;
- stone category;
- setting type;
- estimated dimensions;
- estimated weight.

Gunakan label estimated bila bukan hasil perhitungan CAD/engineering tervalidasi.

Jangan mengklaim sertifikasi batu atau kadar logam.

==================================================
COMPONENT CONTRIBUTION
==================================================

Contoh:

- setting;
- clasp;
- chain;
- pendant;
- stone seat;
- decorative element.

Semua berada dalam plugin.
