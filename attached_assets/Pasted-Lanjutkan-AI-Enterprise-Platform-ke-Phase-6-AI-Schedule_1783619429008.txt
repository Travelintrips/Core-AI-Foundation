Tujuan Phase 6:
Membuat Scheduler & Automation Engine agar AI Platform bisa menjalankan pekerjaan berdasarkan waktu, interval, cron, deadline, SLA, atau event follow-up.

PENTING:
Jangan rombak Job Engine, Dispatcher, Cluster, Event Bus, Creative AI, Workforce, Queue, atau Client Review.
Jangan membuat Marketplace, Billing, Video, Voice, OCR, atau Plugin.
Semua perubahan harus additive, modular, backward compatible, OpenAPI-first, dan wajib lolos pnpm run verify.

Tugas utama:

3. Buat service `aiSchedulerService`

* createSchedule()
* updateSchedule()
* pauseSchedule()
* resumeSchedule()
* cancelSchedule()
* calculateNextRun()
* tick()
* executeDueSchedules()
* recordRun()

4. Buat Scheduler Runtime
   Config:

* AI_SCHEDULER_ENABLED
* AI_SCHEDULER_POLL_INTERVAL_MS
* AI_SCHEDULER_TIMEZONE

Aturan:

* dev boleh auto-start
* production default false jika env tidak diset
* tidak boleh double-start
* tick concurrency-safe
* graceful shutdown
* error satu schedule tidak mematikan scheduler
