TEAM 25 — INTERIOR DESIGN DOMAIN PLUGIN

BRANCH WAJIB:
feature/team-25-interior-plugin

==================================================
TUJUAN
==================================================

Bangun Interior Design Plugin di atas Universal Design Platform.

Semua field, artifact, workflow, property, dan component khusus interior harus
berada di plugin.

==================================================
WORKFLOW
==================================================

Minimal:

1. Brief
2. Site/Space Information
3. Style Research
4. Moodboard
5. Space Planning
6. Material Direction
7. Lighting Direction
8. Furniture Selection
9. Visualization
10. Review
11. Documentation
12. Export

==================================================
BRIEF
==================================================

Contoh:

- space type;
- dimensions;
- occupants;
- style preference;
- functional requirements;
- budget range;
- location/climate;
- existing conditions;
- color preference;
- material preference;
- lighting needs;
- accessibility;
- sustainability.
