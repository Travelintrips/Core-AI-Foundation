====================================================================
TUJUAN
======

Saat ini Job Engine sudah lengkap tetapi masih bersifat manual.

Known limitation:

* claimJob() hanya berjalan jika dipanggil.
* Belum ada background dispatcher.
* Belum ada worker runtime.
* Queue belum memproses job secara otomatis.

Target Phase 5.1:

Mengubah AI Job Engine menjadi Automatic Worker Runtime.

====================================================================
BAGIAN 1
WORKER DISPATCHER
=================

Buat service:

jobDispatcherService

Method:

start()

stop()

tick()

dispatch()

recover()

shutdown()

Dispatcher berjalan menggunakan polling interval yang dapat dikonfigurasi.

Default:

5000 ms

Dispatcher tidak boleh dijalankan dua kali.

====================================================================
BAGIAN 2
AUTO DISPATCH
=============

Dispatcher setiap tick harus:

1.

cek queue paused?

↓

ya

↓

skip

2.

ambil worker idle

3.

claim job

menggunakan claimJob()

4.

jalankan executeJob()

5.

completeJob()

atau

retryJob()

atau

failed

====================================================================
BAGIAN 3
WORKER RUNTIME
==============

Tambahkan runtime worker.

Saat startup:

register worker

heartbeat

status idle

Saat processing:

status busy

running_jobs++

Saat selesai:

running_jobs--

status idle

Saat shutdown:

status offline

====================================================================
BAGIAN 4
STALE WORKER RECOVERY
=====================

Tambahkan recovery.

Jika worker:

heartbeat timeout

↓

offline

Jika worker masih punya running job

↓

release lock

↓

retry

atau

failed

Audit log wajib dibuat.

==================

running

↓

retry

atau

failed

↓

release worker

↓

audit
