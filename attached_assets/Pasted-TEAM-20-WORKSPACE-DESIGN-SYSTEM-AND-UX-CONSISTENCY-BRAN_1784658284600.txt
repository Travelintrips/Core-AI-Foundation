==================================================
DENSITY
==================================================

Dukung minimal:

- comfortable;
- compact.

Jangan membuat puluhan variant.

==================================================
STATUS VISUALS
==================================================

Status harus konsisten untuk:

- draft;
- generating;
- ready;
- in review;
- approved;
- revision requested;
- failed;
- archived;
- unavailable;
- read-only.

Status mapping harus berasal dari canonical mapping atau adapter.

Jangan membuat status bisnis baru.

==================================================
RESPONSIVE
==================================================

Definisikan pola:

Desktop:
- multi-panel;
- toolbar horizontal;
- status bar.

Tablet:
- collapsible panels;
- compact toolbar.

Mobile:
- canvas/viewer utama;
- property/layer/review via sheet/tab;
- no horizontal page overflow;
- touch target memadai.

==================================================
THEME
==================================================

- light mode;
- dark mode;
- high contrast consideration;
- reduced motion;
- browser zoom 200%;
- readable disabled state;
- no hard-coded arbitrary color bila token tersedia.

==================================================
ACCESSIBILITY AUDIT
==================================================

Pastikan primitive:

- focus-visible;
- aria-label;
- tooltip bukan satu-satunya label;
- status tidak hanya warna;
- minimum touch size;
- logical focus order;
- screen-reader labels;
- keyboard usable;
- motion optional.
