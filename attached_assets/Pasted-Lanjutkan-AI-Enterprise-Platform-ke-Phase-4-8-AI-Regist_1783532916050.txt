Lanjutkan AI Enterprise Platform ke Phase 4.8: AI Registry Enterprise & Digital Workforce Foundation.

PENTING:
Jangan mengubah arsitektur yang sudah ada.
Jangan rewrite fitur yang sudah stabil.
Semua implementasi harus additive, modular, backward compatible, dan enterprise-grade.

====================================================================
VISI
====

AI Platform ini tidak lagi diposisikan sebagai sekumpulan AI Agent.

Mulai Phase 4.8 seluruh AI diperlakukan sebagai Digital Workforce.

Artinya setiap AI memiliki:

* Department
* Position
* Skills
* Capability
* Supervisor
* Performance
* Cost Center
* Working Hours
* Status
* Permissions
* Tool Access

Creative AI hanyalah Department pertama.

Di masa depan akan ada:

Creative Department
Marketing Department
Finance Department
HR Department
Tax Department
Legal Department
Sales Department
Logistics Department
Customs Department
Trading Department
Procurement Department
Analytics Department
