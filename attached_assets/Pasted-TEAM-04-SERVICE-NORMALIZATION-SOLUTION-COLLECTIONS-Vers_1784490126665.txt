==================================================
KEY DEFINITIONS
==================================================

Service Record:

An existing purchasable or historical service in the current database.

Canonical Service Concept:

A stable customer-facing concept representing one or more related service records.

Alias:

An alternate name, legacy label, typo, or commonly used term that resolves to a canonical concept.

Duplicate Candidate:

A service suspected of overlapping with another service but not yet approved as a true duplicate.

Normalization Mapping:

A deterministic relationship between an existing service and a canonical service concept.

Solution Collection:

A curated, deterministic grouping of services intended to solve a broader customer outcome.

Examples:

Brand Launch Essentials

Marketing Content Starter

Business Presentation Package

Product Packaging Collection

Do not treat a solution collection as an order bundle unless specifically approved.

A collection is initially a discovery grouping.

It must not automatically change pricing or create a combined order.
