PHASE 3 — BUDGET FAIL-CLOSED POLICY
==================================================

Perbaiki checkDesignBudget agar tidak universally fail-open.

Required behavior:

soft_warn:
- DB/service unavailable dapat menghasilkan warning/unavailable;
- execution policy dapat memutuskan apakah tetap berjalan.

hard_block:
- failure membaca budget harus fail-closed;
- jangan mengembalikan allowed.

require_approval:
- failure harus menghasilkan approval_required atau unavailable;
- jangan otomatis allowed.

Gunakan structured status:

allowed
warning
blocked
approval_required
unavailable

Tambahkan machine-readable reason.

Tambahkan tests:

- soft warning DB failure;
- hard block DB failure;
- require approval DB failure;
- unavailable tidak dianggap allowed.
