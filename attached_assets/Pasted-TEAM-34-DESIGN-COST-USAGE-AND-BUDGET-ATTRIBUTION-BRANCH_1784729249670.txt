TUJUAN
==================================================

Bangun attribution layer agar setiap design execution dapat ditelusuri biaya dan
pemakaiannya berdasarkan:

- order;
- tenant;
- project;
- workflow;
- stage;
- artifact;
- capability;
- agent;
- provider;
- model;
- job;
- retry;
- export/render operation.

Gunakan AI Cost Service dan ai_cost_records existing.

Jangan membuat sistem billing atau cost ledger kedua.
