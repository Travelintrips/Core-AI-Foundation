TEAM 33 — UNIVERSAL DESIGN QUALITY ASSURANCE ENGINE

BRANCH WAJIB:
feature/team-33-design-quality-engine

==================================================
TUJUAN
==================================================

Bangun Quality Assurance Engine universal untuk memvalidasi artifact dan output
design sebelum review atau export.

QA Engine harus mendukung:

- schema validation;
- artifact completeness;
- workflow requirement;
- technical metadata;
- output format;
- resolution;
- accessibility;
- brand constraints;
- compliance contribution;
- plugin-specific rules;
- AI-assisted checks melalui adapter.

Jangan membuat compliance certification palsu.

==================================================

- existing Creative AI QC agent;
- image QC score;
- document validator;
- presentation OOXML validator;
- artifact statuses;
- guardrails;
- review system;
- plugin framework;
- capability matrix;
- existing validation utilities.

==================================================
RULE REGISTRY
==================================================

Rule dapat berasal dari:

- core;
- workflow;
- plugin;
- export format;
- organization policy;
- brand policy.

Registry harus:

- unique rule ID;
- versioned;
- deterministic order;
- capability requirement;
- applicability;
- severity;
- auto-fix declaration;
- source attribution.

==================================================
AI-ASSISTED QA
==================================================

AI check harus melalui Team 31/AI execution adapter.

AI result tidak boleh dianggap fakta absolut.

AI finding harus memiliki:

- confidence;
- reason;
- evidence;
- limitation;
- model provenance;
- human review recommendation bila perlu.

==================================================
SCORING
==================================================

Scoring harus:

- deterministic untuk rule-based checks;
- transparan;
- configurable;
- tidak menghapus blocking finding karena average score tinggi;
- membedakan unavailable check dari passed check.
